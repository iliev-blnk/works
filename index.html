  • throw

    Live visuals, projection mapping and light sync for small venues. Two decks of sound-reactive shader scenes, any number of projectors pinned by their corners on the wall, Art-Net out to lights and moving heads, a phone remote and a 3D previs. Runs in a browser on one laptop with no internet.

    Renders any web page to frame-exact video. The throw demo video is made with it.

    Turns a phone into a remote for any web page by scanning a QR code. throw's phone remote runs on it.

    A live digital twin of Istanbul Airport's airspace from open ADS-B data, with landing-time predictions. Rust, Python and TypeScript.
